Is a live human here?

Cardio liveness authentication. A specialized security layer that proves a live human is holding the device — passkeys prove a device was unlocked; Easy Peasy proves a live human is present.

  • Cryptographic Attestation Token (CAT), HMAC-SHA256
  • Plugs into Okta as a custom authentication factor
  • Server-side local models — biometrics never leave our infrastructure
