Truth AI

Every AI claim, receipted. Proven misstatements, paid automatically.

AI systems fabricate citations and tell users they are mistaken. Truth AI is the accountability layer:

  • Attest — every consequential claim logged as a signed attestation: claim, sources, method, timestamp.
  • Verify — the citation checker re-fetches every source. Dead link, fabricated quote, provider-owned source: fail.
  • Pay — a failed verdict triggers an automatic payout through Stripe Connect. No human in the loop.

Positioned as proactive risk mitigation: never be the next Mata v. Avianca.

Status: working prototype. Payouts activate on Stripe Connect onboarding.
